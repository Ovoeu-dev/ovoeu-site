Bearing

Made to disappear under a work glove.

A low-set oval on a knife-edge band. The stone drops as far into the shank as the setting allows, so nothing catches. Built for a hand that works.

The story it carries is about not taking the ring off. Everything decorative was removed until only that idea was left.

Threshold

Three stones: what was, what is, what is still ahead.

A trilogy set with deliberate asymmetry. The outer stones are cut differently from the centre, so the eye reads a sequence rather than a symmetry.

The story is a doorway. It was designed for the moment before a decision, not after it.

SettingShared-prong trilogy

Edition 03

Quiet Signet

Engraved inside only, where no one else reads it.

A signet with a flush-inset diamond and a flat face left deliberately plain. All the ornament lives on the inner wall. A date, a line, a name, cut by hand.

The story is privacy. A piece that says nothing outward and everything inward.
